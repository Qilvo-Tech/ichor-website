Privacy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

We keep a simple page-view total to see how much this site is used. The counter doesn't identify you or use cookies or browser storage.

If you have a privacy question or want to make a request, email leo@qilvo.games.

This page explains how we handle privacy on ichor.qilvo.games. Game accounts and other platforms have their own privacy information.

A simple page count

When you load the home page or this page, our server can add one to the total. It uses the page request you're already making, so your browser doesn't send an extra analytics request.

All the counter saves is that number. It doesn't save IP addresses, browser details, page addresses, timestamps, or a history of visits. We can't use it to recognise you when you come back or follow you to another website.

It's a rough count: refreshing a page can add another view, automated visits can count, and a page loaded entirely from your browser's cache may be missed. You can see the total here. We keep it for as long as we use the counter.

If your browser sends a Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control signal, we skip counting that visit. We don't set a cookie to remember this choice.

Hosting and security

Cloudflare hosts this site and helps protect it. To send you a page, it handles information such as your IP address, the page you asked for, and your browser's request headers. None of this goes into the counter.

Our website doesn't keep individual analytics logs. Cloudflare may process service and security data or set security cookies when checking suspicious traffic. Its privacy policy explains how it uses that data and decides how long to keep it.

Cloudflare's network is worldwide, so data may be processed outside the European Economic Area. Its Data Processing Addendum describes the safeguards for those transfers.

Why we do this

We want to keep the site working safely and get a general sense of how much it's used. For the personal data involved in serving and protecting the site, we rely on legitimate interests under Article 6(1)(f) of the GDPR. The counter itself keeps only an anonymous total.

We don't build visitor profiles, use the count for personalised ads or decisions about individuals, or share visitor information with another analytics service.

Your rights and choices

You can email leo@qilvo.games about your data. Where GDPR rights apply, you can ask to access, correct or delete your personal data, limit how it's used, or object to processing based on legitimate interests.

We can't pick out or remove an individual visit from the counter because it only holds a total, with no record of who visited.

You also have the right to make a complaint to Estonia's Data Protection Inspectorate or your local data protection authority.

Links to other platforms

If you follow a link to Steam, Patreon, Discord, or another website, that service's own privacy policy applies. We link to these platforms without embedding their tracking scripts here.
