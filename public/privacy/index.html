A simple page count

When the server delivers the home page or this privacy page, it may add one to a shared total. Counting happens on the server as part of that normal request. Your browser does not send a separate analytics request.

The counter stores only the total number of page views. It does not keep IP addresses, browser details, page URLs, timestamps, or individual visit records. It cannot recognise you on another visit or follow you between websites.

Reloads can count again, some automated requests can count, and pages restored entirely from your browser cache may not count. The aggregate total is public and is kept for the lifetime of the counter.

If your browser sends a Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control opt-out signal, we skip counting that page request. We do not store a cookie to remember this preference.

Hosting and security

Cloudflare serves and protects this website. Delivering a page involves processing network information such as your IP address, the requested address, and browser request headers. The counter does not copy this information into its stored total.

The website application does not write individual analytics logs. Cloudflare may process service and security data or set security cookies when checking suspicious traffic. Its privacy policy explains that processing and the criteria used for retention.

Cloudflare operates a global network, so processing may take place outside the European Economic Area. Its Data Processing Addendum sets out the applicable safeguards for international transfers.

Why we process this information

Our legitimate interests under Article 6(1)(f) of the GDPR are to operate a reliable, secure public website and understand its overall use without identifying visitors. We limit counting to the normal page-delivery request and immediately retain only an aggregate number.

We do not use the count for personalised advertising, visitor profiles, or decisions about individuals. We do not share visitor information with an additional analytics service.

Your rights and choices

Where applicable, you can request access to, correction, deletion, or restriction of personal data, and object to processing based on legitimate interests. Contact leo@qilvo.games. We cannot extract a person's visits from the anonymous total because it contains no visitor records.

You can also complain to the Estonian Data Protection Inspectorate or your local data protection authority.

Links to other platforms

Steam, Patreon, Discord, and other external links open services operated by their respective providers. Their privacy policies apply when you visit them. We do not embed their tracking scripts on this website.
